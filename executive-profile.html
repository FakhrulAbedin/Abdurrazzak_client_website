Executive Overview

Leadership Across Trade, Credit, Compliance & Operations

Md. Abdur Razzak is a banking professional with more than 21 years of experience and progressive responsibility at Islami Bank Bangladesh PLC. His experience covers foreign exchange, corporate credit, trade finance, banking operations, internal audit, transaction monitoring, portfolio management, AML/CFT and customer relationship management.

His current responsibilities include portfolio-quality monitoring, trade proposal review and TBML risk assessment, LC and export-documentation supervision, SWIFT and FX operations, corporate-financing evaluation, due diligence, compliance review and corporate relationship management.

Career Progression

Increasing Scope Across Banking Functions

He started in 2005 as a Probationary Officer in customer relationship management before moving into foreign exchange, investment and general banking. He later progressed through Senior Principal Officer and FAVP leadership roles and currently serves as FAVP (Export In-charge).
